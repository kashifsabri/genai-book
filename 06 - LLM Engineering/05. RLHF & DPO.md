# 5. RLHF & DPO

## Introduction

After Supervised Fine-Tuning (SFT), the model becomes much better at following instructions.

However, another problem still exists.

Sometimes the model may:

- Give unsafe answers
- Produce unhelpful responses
- Be rude
- Give long answers when a short answer is better
- Choose a poor response even when it knows a better one

To improve the quality of responses, another training stage is used.

This stage is called **RLHF (Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback).**

Today, many newer models are also trained using **DPO (Direct Preference Optimization)**, which is a simpler alternative.

---

# 5.1 Why Do We Need Another Training Stage?

Imagine asking an AI:

```text
Write a short email requesting one day of leave.
```

Suppose the model generates:

Response A

```text
I need leave.
```

Response B

```text
Dear Manager,

I would like to request one day's leave due to a personal commitment.

Thank you.
```

Both responses are correct.

But most people would agree that **Response B** is much better.

The model must learn **which good answer people actually prefer**.

This is exactly what RLHF and DPO teach.

---

# 5.2 What is RLHF?

**RLHF** stands for:

**Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback**

The idea is simple.

Humans compare multiple responses from the model and choose the better one.

Example:

Question

```text
Explain Artificial Intelligence.
```

Response A

```text
AI is computers.
```

Response B

```text
Artificial Intelligence is the ability of computers to perform tasks that normally require human intelligence, such as learning and problem solving.
```

Human evaluators choose:

```text
Response B
```

The model gradually learns that responses like **B** are preferred.

---

# 5.3 How Does RLHF Work?

The process looks like this.

```text
Pretrained Model
        │
        ▼
Supervised Fine-Tuning
        │
        ▼
Generate Multiple Answers
        │
        ▼
Humans Rank the Answers
        │
        ▼
Model Learns Human Preferences
```

Instead of learning from only one correct answer,

the model learns from **human preferences**.

---

# 5.4 Example

Suppose the user asks:

```text
How do I boil an egg?
```

The model generates two answers.

Answer A

```text
Put the egg in hot water.
```

Answer B

```text
1. Place the egg in a pot.
2. Cover it with water.
3. Bring the water to a boil.
4. Cook for about 8–10 minutes.
5. Cool the egg in cold water before peeling.
```

Most people prefer **Answer B** because it is clearer.

The model learns to generate answers like B more often.

---

# 5.5 What is a Reward Model?

During RLHF,

human rankings are first collected.

Then another model, called a **Reward Model**, learns those preferences.

Instead of asking humans every time,

the Reward Model estimates which response is better.

Example:

```text
Response A

↓

Reward = 0.35
```

```text
Response B

↓

Reward = 0.92
```

The training process encourages the LLM to produce responses with higher rewards.

---

# 5.6 What is DPO?

**DPO** stands for:

**Direct Preference Optimization**

DPO is a newer training method.

Instead of using a separate Reward Model and reinforcement learning,

it learns directly from human preference data.

Suppose humans choose:

```text
Answer B
```

instead of

```text
Answer A
```

DPO directly updates the model to prefer **Answer B**.

This makes the training process much simpler.

---

# 5.7 RLHF vs DPO

| RLHF | DPO |
|------|-----|
| Uses a Reward Model | No Reward Model |
| Uses Reinforcement Learning | Directly learns from preferences |
| More complex training | Simpler training |
| Used in many early LLMs | Common in many newer LLMs |

Both methods have the same goal.

They teach the model to produce responses that people prefer.

---

# 5.8 Real-World Example

Imagine two chefs cooking the same dish.

Both dishes are edible.

Now customers taste both dishes.

Most customers choose one dish over the other.

The chef learns from customer feedback and improves future dishes.

RLHF and DPO work in the same way.

The model improves by learning what humans prefer.

---

# 5.9 Does RLHF Teach New Knowledge?

No.

Most knowledge was already learned during pretraining.

RLHF and DPO mainly improve:

- Helpfulness
- Politeness
- Safety
- Response quality
- Instruction following

Think of the training stages like this.

```text
Pretraining

↓

Learns language
```

```text
SFT

↓

Learns instructions
```

```text
RLHF / DPO

↓

Learns human preferences
```

---

# Common Beginner Mistakes

### Mistake 1

Thinking RLHF teaches new facts.

It mainly teaches better behavior and response quality.

---

### Mistake 2

Thinking every modern model still uses RLHF.

Many newer models use DPO because it is simpler to train.

---

### Mistake 3

Thinking RLHF replaces SFT.

It doesn't.

RLHF (or DPO) usually comes after Supervised Fine-Tuning.

---

# Key Takeaways

- RLHF stands for Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback.
- Humans compare multiple responses and choose the better one.
- RLHF teaches the model to produce responses that people prefer.
- DPO is a simpler alternative that learns directly from preference data.
- Both methods improve helpfulness, safety, and response quality.
- RLHF and DPO usually come after Supervised Fine-Tuning.
