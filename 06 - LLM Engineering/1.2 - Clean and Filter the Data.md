# Step 2 — Clean and Filter the Data

After collecting massive amounts of text, the data is **not** ready for training.

Raw data from the internet contains many problems.

For example:


- Duplicate articles
- Spam
- Advertisements
- Broken HTML pages
- Random symbols
- Incomplete sentences
- Low-quality content

If we train an LLM on poor-quality data, it will learn poor-quality patterns.

This is why data cleaning is one of the most important steps in LLM training.

---

# 1.11 Why Can't We Use Raw Data?

Imagine downloading thousands of documents from the internet.

Some documents may contain:

```text
Buy now!!!

Click here!!!

$$$$$$$$$

asdfghjkl

%%%%%%%
```

Some web pages may contain only navigation menus.

Others may contain advertisements instead of useful information.

Training on this data would confuse the model.

Instead of learning language, it would waste time learning useless patterns.

---

# 1.12 Common Data Cleaning Steps

Before training, engineers clean the dataset.

Some common steps include:

### Remove Duplicate Data

Sometimes the same article appears thousands of times on different websites.

Training on duplicate content gives too much importance to the same information.

Duplicates are removed to improve data quality.

---

### Remove Spam

Spam includes content such as:

```text
Win $1000 Now!!

Click Here!!

Free Gift!!
```

This type of content is usually removed.

---

### Remove Corrupted Data

Some files may contain:

- Broken characters
- Invalid text
- Encoding errors

Example:

```text
�����abc##@!!
```

Such data is discarded because it provides little or no useful information.

---

### Remove Very Short Documents

Documents with only a few words often don't provide enough context for learning.

Example:

```text
Hello
```

or

```text
Good Morning
```

These are usually not useful for training a large language model.

---

### Filter Harmful or Low-Quality Content

Depending on the model and its purpose, engineers may filter:

- Duplicate content
- Extremely low-quality text
- Machine-generated junk
- Content that doesn't meet the project's data quality standards

The exact filtering process varies between organizations and models.

---

# 1.13 Data Quality is More Important Than Data Quantity

Suppose you have two datasets.

Dataset A

```text
10 TB

Poor Quality
```

Dataset B

```text
5 TB

High Quality
```

Which one is better?

Usually,

the smaller, cleaner dataset produces better models.

A model learns from the quality of the data, not just the quantity.

---

# 1.14 Real-World Example

Imagine preparing for an important exam.

Would you study from:

- Correct textbooks
- Trusted notes
- Good reference books

or from:

- Random social media posts
- Incorrect notes
- Duplicate pages

Most students would choose the first option.

LLMs also learn better from clean, reliable data.

---

# Engineering Note

Large AI companies spend enormous effort on data preparation.

In many real-world LLM projects, **data collection and cleaning take more time than model training itself**.

A better dataset often improves model performance more than simply increasing the model size.
