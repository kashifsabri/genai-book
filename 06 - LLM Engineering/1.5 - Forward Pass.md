# Step 5 — Forward Pass

The embeddings are now ready.

The next step is to pass them through the Transformer.

This process is called the **Forward Pass**.

A forward pass simply means:

> **The input travels through the neural network to produce an output.**

During this step, **the model does not learn yet**.

It only makes a prediction.

---

# 1.30 What Happens During a Forward Pass?

Suppose our input sentence is:

```text
I love AI
```

After tokenization and embeddings, it becomes:

```text
Embedding 1

Embedding 2

Embedding 3
```

These embeddings enter the Transformer.

Inside the Transformer, several operations happen.

```text
Embeddings
      │
      ▼
Self-Attention
      │
      ▼
Feed Forward Network
      │
      ▼
Residual Connection
      │
      ▼
Layer Normalization
      │
      ▼
Next Transformer Block
```

This process repeats through every Transformer block.

Finally, the model produces an output.

---

# 1.31 What Does the Transformer Do?

Remember,

the Transformer does not simply pass the embeddings forward.

It improves them.

For each token, it asks questions like:

- Which other words are important?
- Which words should I pay attention to?
- What is the meaning of this word in this sentence?

This is exactly what **Self-Attention** does.

By the end of the forward pass,

every token has a much richer representation than before.

---

# 1.32 Example

Consider the sentence:

```text
The animal didn't cross the road because it was tired.
```

What does **"it"** refer to?

The Transformer uses Self-Attention to understand that:

```text
it

↓

animal
```

instead of

```text
road
```

This understanding happens during the forward pass.

---

# 1.33 Output of the Forward Pass

After passing through all Transformer layers,

the model produces a prediction.

Suppose the input is:

```text
I love
```

The model might predict:

| Token | Probability |
|--------|------------:|
| AI | 0.82 |
| Python | 0.10 |
| Football | 0.05 |
| Coffee | 0.03 |

Notice that the model doesn't directly say:

```text
AI
```

Instead, it gives a **probability** for every token in its vocabulary.

The token with the highest probability is the prediction.

---

# 1.34 Is the Prediction Always Correct?

No.

During the beginning of training,

the model makes many mistakes.

For example,

Input:

```text
The capital of France is
```

Correct Answer:

```text
Paris
```

The model might predict:

```text
London
```

This is completely normal.

The model hasn't learned enough yet.

The next steps will teach the model how to correct these mistakes.

---

# 1.35 Real-World Example

Imagine you're taking a multiple-choice exam.

Question:

```text
The largest planet is:
```

Options:

- Earth
- Mars
- Jupiter
- Venus

Before checking the answer,

you first make your prediction.

This prediction is similar to the **forward pass**.

You haven't learned anything yet.

You've simply given your answer.

# Engineering Note

During training, millions of sentences are processed together in small groups called **batches**.

Instead of processing one sentence at a time,

modern GPUs process hundreds or even thousands of examples simultaneously.

This makes training much faster.

We'll learn more about **batches** in a later chapter.
