# What's Coming Next?

So far, we've focused on the **model itself**.

But LLMs have one major limitation.

They only know what was learned during training.

For example,

if you ask:

```text
What is my company's leave policy?
```

the model cannot answer because that information was never part of its training data.

So how do companies build AI systems that can answer questions using:

- Company documents
- PDFs
- Databases
- Websites
- Internal knowledge

The answer is:

**Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG).**

In the next phase, we'll learn how to combine LLMs with external knowledge so they can answer questions using your own data.

Welcome to the world of **RAG**.
